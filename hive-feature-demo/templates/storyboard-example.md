# Feature demo: Google Chat can prepare emails and meetings (19.5 s)

**Feature (release note):** Asking Sayya in Google Chat to send an email or book a meeting prepares it for approval instead of failing. Mentat's hourly sync acts on bounces and unsubscribes again and checks only campaign people against HubSpot. A blog post read from a Google Doc can be submitted for review.

**UX part to show:** the Chat ask turning into items for approval, which you approve in Hive's Review queue. The Mentat sync is a backend fix and gets a short "also in this release" card, as does the blog submission.

**Frame:** the Hive shell rebuilt from the screenshots (sidebar, top bar, footer; IBM Plex; Hive's dark palette). A caption pill at the bottom steps through the story. A camera zooms in on wherever the action is. The data is invented: Priya Raman at Northwind Labs.
